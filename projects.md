## Autonomous Materials Discovery {#autonomous-materials-discovery}

Machine learning combined with robotic experimentation for controlled, high-throughput synthesis of semiconductor nanomaterials.

The project explores how automated experimental platforms and data-driven models can work together to accelerate materials discovery.

## Perovskite Photovoltaics {#perovskite-photovoltaics}

Machine learning approaches for understanding performance, reproducibility, and optimization in perovskite solar cells and modules.

Topics include failed experiments, dataset composition, uncertainty, experimental variability, and model interpretability.

## Hydrogen Materials {#hydrogen-materials}

Research on nanomaterials and catalysts relevant to hydrogen production and storage.

## Deep Eutectic Solvents {#deep-eutectic-solvents}

Experimental investigation of deep eutectic solvents as platforms for the synthesis and processing of nanomaterials.

The project examines alternative solvent systems and their influence on material formation and properties.
