<section class="section intro-grid">

    <p class="section-label">About</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <h2>From data to experiments.</h2>
    <p>
      PANDAS Lab brings together machine learning, materials science, chemical
      engineering, and laboratory automation to accelerate materials research.
      We are particularly interested in the connection between predictive models
      and experiments: using data to decide what to make, learning from what
      happens in the laboratory, and iterating toward better materials and processes.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>
